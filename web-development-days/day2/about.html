About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a simple app for jotting down ideas, reminders and to-dos the moment they come to mind. It keeps all your notes in one place, so nothing gets forgotten before you can act on it.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Open the Home page.
  2. Type your idea into the “New note” box.
  3. Press the Add Note button.
  4. Read your notes in the list below the form.

Features

  • Add a note in seconds
  • See all your notes in one list
  • Live count of how many notes you have
  • Simple, distraction-free layout

Keyboard shortcuts

Shortcut Action
Enter Add the note you have typed
Tab Move to the next field or button
Shift + Tab Move to the previous field or button
